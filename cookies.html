Cookie Policy

One essential cookie

We use one cookie, session, to keep you logged in and to protect forms against forgery. It ends when you close the browser, or after 30 days if you tick "Remember me".

Preferences on your device

If you accept, we save your light or dark theme choice in your browser's storage. It is never sent to our server. Choose "Essential only" and it is not saved.

No tracking

There are no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. Map images load from Esri and fonts load from Google Fonts, so those services see your IP address.

What we store about you

Your name, username, email (optional), a hashed password (never the password itself), your role, the requests you make and the activity history. Alerts for critical requests include the shelter, its location, the sender's name and the time.
